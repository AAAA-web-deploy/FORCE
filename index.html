“Maybe we should start a competitor to Amazon.”

On October 15, 2026, Elon Musk posted those words during a discussion about restrictions on AI shopping agents. He offered no launch plan, leaving an intriguing possibility open.

Musk Gravity begins with that possibility.

If Musk followed through, what might he build? Imagine a marketplace designed around intelligent assistants from the beginning: describe what you need, let an agent explore the options, and move from intention to purchase with fewer obstacles.

Musk Gravity is our independent interpretation of that imagined future—a company concept shaped around the possibility of a new challenger entering commerce.

The river in our identity represents the movement of goods, ideas, and opportunity. Gravity represents the attraction that could draw people toward a different destination. Together, they express our central idea: even an established current can change direction.

FORCE is the ticker of Musk Gravity’s Ethereum community token. It gives this narrative a shared identity for people interested in the possibility Musk raised. Its meaning comes from movement: the force required to challenge familiar systems and imagine another way forward.

The remark supplies the spark. Musk Gravity gives the community’s imagination a name.

The force that moves the river.
